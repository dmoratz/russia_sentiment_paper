Country-level clustering check, H2 and H3a RDID estimates
=========================================================

Wild cluster restricted (WCR) bootstrap: Webb six-point weights, null imposed,
bootstrap_type = fnw11, B = 99999.

Clustering affects inference only, so the point estimate is shared across the
two clustering levels. Sources are nested within countries; country clustering
is the more conservative level when shocks are country-wide.
